# Robot inspection workspace

Mode: Operate. The user's instruction to build end to end settles the design workflow without a separate direction approval. Seven grounded directions considered: machining console, Onshape inspector, robotics lab notebook, precision product configurator, fabrication workbench, scientific instrument panel, museum exploded specimen. The fourth is the chosen direction: a light gray 3D inspection field, compact dark blue header, quiet component tree and precise joint controls. Amber identifies follower prints, blue identifies leader prints, graphite and metal identify purchased hardware. Measurements use tabular numerals. The CAD export is the visual centerpiece; there are no generated mock robot images. The cloud/quarry challengers do not serve an engineering inspection task; their whitespace discipline is retained. Mobile inspection preserves the viewport first and puts controls below it.
