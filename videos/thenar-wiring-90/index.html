05

PCA9685 signals → MG996R servos.

FOLLOWER ARM

Signal leads

PCA outputs CH0–CH5 go to the six servo signal wires, one joint per channel. Match signal, + and − markings on your actual board.

Servo power

Separate regulated 6 V + goes through a fuse and switch to servo V+. Its − goes to servo GND. Pi and servo grounds share a reference.

Never connect the 6 V positive rail to Pi 5 V, Pi 3V3, or PCA9685 VCC.

01

Use only one ESP32.

It reads the leader. The Pi reads USB data and controls the follower over I²C.

The Pi GPIO does not power or directly pulse six servos.
02

Connect six leader sensors.

AS5600 SDA/SCL pairs go to mux channels 0–5; share 3V3 and GND.

Each AS5600 uses address 0x36; the TCA9548A isolates the six copies.
03

Plug the leader into the Pi.

Mux SDA → ESP32 GPIO21; SCL → GPIO22. Use one USB data cable.

The ESP32 runs leader firmware only. Pi USB-A is data, not servo power.
04

Wire Pi header to PCA9685.

Physical pins 1, 6, 3, 5, 11 → VCC, GND, SDA, SCL, OE.

GPIO17/OE HIGH disables PWM. Keep 6V OFF until the Pi program is ready.
05

Connect servo leads and 6V.

PCA channels 0–5 → six servo plugs. External 6V → V+ and GND.

Common ground. Check plug polarity and the board’s current rating; fuse and switch the rail.
06

Test one unloaded joint first.

Monitor the leader, calibrate pulses, then test a single servo before all six.

Current bridge.py expects a second ESP32; a Pi-side PCA9685 driver is required.
